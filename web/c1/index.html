​DECENTRALIZE* IS A MULTIFACETED ARTIST EQUALLY SKILLED IN TRADITIONAL AND DIGITAL ART. WITH A PASSION FOR CREATIVITY AND A THIRST FOR EXPERIMENTATION, HE CONSTANTLY PUSHES THE BOUNDARIES OF WHAT IS POSSIBLE IN HIS ART.

AS A PAINTER, DECENTRALIZE* HAS A UNIQUE AND DISTINCTIVE STYLE THAT IS INFLUENCED BY A WIDE RANGE OF SOURCES, FROM CLASSICAL ART TO CONTEMPORARY POP CULTURE. HIS PAINTINGS ARE VIBRANT AND LIVELY, FILLED WITH BOLD COLORS AND DYNAMIC COMPOSITIONS.

AS A DIGITAL ARTIST, DECENTRALIZE* IS EQUALLY AT HOME CREATING INTRICATE AND DETAILED ILLUSTRATIONS, AS WELL AS MORE ABSTRACT AND EXPERIMENTAL PIECES. HIS WORK IN THIS MEDIUM IS OFTEN CHARACTERIZED BY A FOCUS ON FIGURATIVE SCENES WITH WEIRD BUT ALMOST ORGANIC GEOMETRIES AND A FASCINATION WITH THE INTERSECTION OF ART AND TECHNOLOGY, BOTH AS A MEDIUM AND A SUBJECT.

WHETHER WORKING WITH A BRUSH OR A DIGITAL PEN, DECENTRALIZE* ALWAYS STRIVES TO CREATE EXCITING, ENGAGING, AND THOUGHT-PROVOKING ART. HIS WORK REFLECTS HIS LOVE FOR CREATIVITY AND HIS UNWAVERING COMMITMENT TO EXPLORING NEW FRONTIERS IN ART.

IN THE SEARCH FOR RAWNESS, MARINATED WITH THE METAMORPHOSIS OF VIRTUAL MAN AND SEASONED WITH SPEED, FUNKY COLORS, DARK EMOTIONS, VIOLENCE , AND TECHNOLOGY.

DECENTRALIZE*​

IS AN ORGANISM TO DESIGN AND BUILD THE NEEDS OF THE 21ST CENTURY WITH DIGITAL TOOLS OVER META-MEDIUM, STARTING FROM PHYSICAL MEDIUMS.

​

WHAT IS " DECENTRALIZE* " ?​

AS WELL AS SERVING AS A SCIENTIFIC TERM FOR BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGIES,

THE DEFINITION LIKEWISE SYMBOLIZES

A SHIFT IN THE CONVENTIONAL DIRECTION AND A PHASE

TO THE NEW ARTIFICIAL PERSONA OF THE INFORMATION AGE.

OPEN-SOURCE TECHNOLOGY EVOLVED FROM HOMO-SAPIENS INTELLIGENCE THAT WAS CLOSED-SOURCE BECAUSE OF THIS DETACHMENT FROM DOGMAS.

ALL IMAGINATIVE -DECENTRALIZED*- ACTS HINGE AROUND THE BELIEF THAT THE HUMAN RACE SEEKS MORE PROFOUND VALUE IN THEIR EXISTENCE.

INVESTIGATING SOCIO-PHYSICS THROUGH INTELLECTUALLY CONSTRUCTED ADVENTUROUS ACTS TO DISCOVER WHY MANKIND STRIVES TO EVOLVE IN THIS DIRECTION AND HOW THIS PROCESS WILL RELY ON THE DAILY LIFE PERSPECTIVE.​
